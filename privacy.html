Privacy

No inbox upload. No tracking. No backend.

Bye Bye Fishing handles the visible message details needed for phishing checks on your device. The boring architecture is the privacy feature.

Effective September 22, 2026 · Publisher: Makio64

Information it handles

On supported webmail pages, it temporarily processes the visible sender name, sender address and domain, email subject, visible message text, link text, and link destinations. Email can contain personal communications and personal, financial, health, or authentication details.

Why it handles that information

Those details are used only to compare the visible message with the packaged safety rules, identify phishing or scam signals, and show an explanation and safer next step. Subjects, bodies, and full link URLs are never saved. Sender addresses are temporary unless you explicitly save a sender-and-site exception as described below.

Information stored on this device

Display preferences, edited safety rules, remembered sender domains, sender-and-site exceptions, and the privacy-setup choice stay in device-local browser extension storage. They remain until you clear or reset them, or remove the extension. They are not placed in browser Sync.

If you confirm Remember this link, the exception saves the sender's email address (when available), sender domain, and destination domain. It does not save the message or full link URL. Remember this sender saves only the sender domain and applies to addresses at that domain. These choices are optional, explain their scope before confirmation, and offer Undo. You can also delete them in Options.

No sharing or human access

Bye Bye Fishing does not send message content or stored choices to the publisher or any third party. It has no developer-operated backend, accounts, analytics, ads, tracking pixels, third-party scripts, or remote code. The publisher cannot read your messages or stored choices.

Permissions and site access

The storage permission retains only the device-local choices described above. Site access is limited to the supported webmail domains listed in the extension manifest and is used only to read the visible signals needed for phishing checks and render warning UI.

Your controls

The Options page lets you remove remembered senders and pages, clear those lists, and restore the default safety rules. Removing the extension deletes its device-local extension storage through the browser.

Limited Use commitment

Bye Bye Fishing's handling of user information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information is handled only to provide the disclosed phishing-protection feature. It is not sold, used for advertising or credit decisions, transferred for unrelated purposes, or made available for human review.

Changes and contact

If data handling changes, the extension will disclose the new practice before it begins and this page will receive a new effective date. Questions and privacy requests can be opened in the public support tracker.
