Privacy

No inbox upload. No tracking. No backend.

Bye Bye Fishing is designed to run locally inside the browser. The boring architecture is the privacy feature.

What the extension reads

On supported webmail pages, it reads visible sender information, visible link destinations, and visible mail body text so it can compare claimed brands and URLs with the local rule list.

What it stores

Settings, edited rules, and locally green-flagged sender domains are stored with the browser extension storage API.

What it does not do

It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share email content, sender addresses, browsing history, analytics events, or personal data.

What it does not use

It does not use remote code, third-party scripts, tracking pixels, cookies, ads, analytics, or a backend service.
