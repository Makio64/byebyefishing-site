Before you download

Is Bye Bye Fishing free?

Yes. The developer preview is free, with no account or subscription. It currently requires manual installation; store releases are pending.

Does it upload or save my email?

No message upload, backend or analytics is used. Visible email is processed locally. Settings and optional sender-and-site exceptions can be saved on your device; message bodies and full URLs are not persisted.

Will it catch every phishing email?

No. It can miss attacks and produce false alarms. It does not inspect attachments, image-only text or QR codes. Treat it as another signal and verify sensitive requests independently.
