Questions before downloading

A few clear answers before you try it.

How the free preview works, what stays on your device and what the extension can—and cannot—check.

Is Bye Bye Fishing free?

Yes. The current developer preview is free to download, with no extension account or subscription. Store releases are still pending.

Which browsers can I use?

Desktop Chrome and Edge use the Chrome-compatible ZIP. Firefox has a separate package for temporary testing. Installed-browser and provider validation remain in progress. Android is experimental, and no Safari or iPhone package is available.

Does it read or upload my email?

It temporarily checks the visible sender, subject, message text and link destinations on supported webmail pages, after you enable local protection. It does not upload messages, send analytics or contact a developer-operated backend.

What is saved on my device?

Display settings, edited rules and optional remembered exceptions are saved in local extension storage. Remembering a sender-and-site exception can save the sender email address, sender domain and destination domain. Message bodies and full link URLs are not persisted.

Does it stop every phishing email?

No. It can miss phishing and show false alarms. It adds visible checks and explanations; it does not authenticate a sender, block every attack, inspect attachments or read QR codes. Open the known site independently before sharing passwords or sending money.

Is this a spam blocker?

No. It looks for phishing and scam signals in visible messages. It does not move newsletters, advertisements or unsolicited mail into a spam folder.

Does it scan the Gmail or Outlook phone app?

No. Native phone apps and browser-based webmail are separate. The preview targets supported webmail pages in a browser; no native email app is scanned.

Why do I need to update the extension?

The packaged list of known phishing URLs expires after 30 days. Updated packages refresh that layer. Other message checks continue when the list expires, and the popup shows the status.

Why is installation manual?

The extension is a developer preview while detection, compatibility and store review work continues. Chrome and Edge require an unpacked local install; Firefox temporary add-ons are removed after a browser restart.

How can I report a problem?

Use the public support tracker linked from the Support page. Share a fictional or fully redacted example. Do not post real emails, passwords, security codes or account information.

Ready to try local email checks?

Choose your desktop browser and follow the manual setup steps.
